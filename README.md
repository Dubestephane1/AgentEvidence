# AgentEvidence — Independent Safety & Governance Review for AI Agents

A productized, independent technical audit service for AI agents and autonomous systems. This repo contains the public-facing landing site, sample reports, and the methodology behind it.

**Live site:** [agentevidence.netlify.app](https://agentevidence.netlify.app)

## What it is

AgentEvidence provides **independent technical safety and governance reviews** for AI agents and autonomous systems. Buyers of AI agents increasingly face vendor security reviews that ask for evidence of safety controls. This service delivers **file:line evidence** of what a system has, what it's missing, and what to fix next — with a **3-day turnaround**.

**Productized offering:** $1,900 per review — includes GitHub read access, structured report, prioritized findings.

## What a review produces

- A clear, structured report with **findings, evidence, and prioritized recommendations**
- **file:line traceability** — evidence tied to actual code locations
- Alignment assessment against **EU AI Act principles** (explicitly *not* formal Art. 43 certification or legal advice)
- A **Checklist A–F methodology** with modular scope (code & modules)

## Why this matters

As AI agents move into production, enterprises need **independent** verification that safety claims hold up — not vendor marketing. This repo shows a real, sellable governance product built around that gap, including concrete sample reports.

## Honest scope

This is an **independent technical assessment** — it is not a formal certification under EU AI Act Art. 43, and it is not legal advice. It tells clients what they have, what they're missing, and what to fix next.
